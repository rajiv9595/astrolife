#### 1. MANGLIK / KUJA DOSHA

**Category:** Marriage
**Tradition:** Parashari Classical (attributed to BPHS)
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Controversial:** YES (scope and cancellation rules debated)

Three independent methods — each evaluates Mars placement from a different reference point:

**Method: LAGNA_CLASSICAL**
- Reference: Ascendant (1st house)
- Affected houses: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12
- Formation: Mars in any affected house from Lagna
- Source: BPHS (attributed, exact verse unverified)

**Method: MOON_REFERENCE**
- Reference: Moon sign
- Affected houses: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12
- Formation: Mars in any affected house from Moon

**Method: VENUS_REFERENCE**
- Reference: Venus sign
- Affected houses: 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, 12
- Formation: Mars in any affected house from Venus

**Cancellation (all methods):**
- Jupiter conjunct Mars → FULL
- Jupiter aspects Mars (Parashari) → FULL
- Mars in own sign → PARTIAL (disputed)

**Mitigation (all methods):**
- Mars exalted → PARTIAL
- Strong Lagna lord → PARTIAL
- Benefic in 7th house → PARTIAL

**Severity:** Categorical (NONE / LOW / MODERATE / HIGH)
- Based on Mars dignity and house position

#### 2. KEMADRUMA DOSHA

**Category:** General
**Tradition:** Parashari Classical
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Controversial:** MODERATE (cancellations vary)

**Formation (strict classical — ALL must be true):**
1. No planet in 2nd house from Moon
2. No planet in 12th house from Moon
3. No planet in kendra (1, 4, 7, 10) from Moon (excluding Moon)

**Cancellation:**
- Jupiter aspects Moon → FULL

**Mitigation:**
- Moon exalted or own sign → PARTIAL
- Venus conjunct Moon → PARTIAL

**Severity:** Categorical
- Moon dignity based
